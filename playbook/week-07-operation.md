# Week 7 — Operation

**Ship:** a weekly operating rhythm you can run after the cohort. Bottleneck named and killed (one).

**War Room:** vanity work, tool-hopping, no numbers.

---

## Lesson 1 — Operators run a week, not a mood

Fixed weekly cadence:

| Block | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Outreach | Fill pipeline (never zero) |
| Sales conversations | Move deals |
| Delivery | Keep promises |
| Admin | Invoices, sheet, taxes-lite |
| War Room / review | Fix the constraint |

If outreach dies because delivery is busy, you have a feast-famine machine. Protect a minimum outbound even with a client (smaller volume, not zero).

---

## Lesson 2 — Scoreboard

Vault `weekly-scorecard.md` is the only dashboard.

Look at: touches → conversations → next steps → offers → cash.

Where the drop is, that is the only place you “work on the business.” New logo, new CRM, new niche is usually cowardice.

---

## Lesson 3 — Poland ops (lite, not legal advice)

This is orientation, not a tax ruling. Confirm with an accountant.

- Testing demand: unregistered activity has a quarterly revenue cap (check current limit).
- When you outgrow it: JDG, ulga na start vs full ZUS — model the real monthly load before you promise retainers you cannot support.
- Invoices, BDO if relevant, RODO if you handle personal data (lead lists).
- Do not give clients legal/tax advice as a service unless that is actually your offer.

Write: how you will get paid (entity), and the date you will talk to an accountant if cash is real.

## Lesson 4 — Kill one bottleneck

Examples: no list, no dials, no price, no deposit, no weekly client update.

Pick one. Change the process. Measure next week.
