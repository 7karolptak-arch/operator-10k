# Week 3 — Pipeline (list)

**Ship:** 200 ICP companies with phone or email (or LinkedIn URL). First 50 touches logged.

**War Room:** list quality, not “I need a better CRM.”

---

## Lesson 1 — Pipeline is a list you work, not a tool you buy

You need: spreadsheet, phone, notes. Optional: simple CRM later.

Columns: company, city, contact name, phone, email, source, last touch, result, next action, date.

Result codes: no answer / gatekeeper / not a fit / send info / meeting / won / lost.

---

## Lesson 2 — Where 200 names come from

Poland-friendly sources: Google Maps, Baza firm, LinkedIn search, industry directories, competitor’s client pages, local Facebook groups **for research not spam**.

Rules:

- Same ICP as Week 1. No “while I was there I added restaurants too.”
- Prefer phone. Email-only lists convert slower for this vehicle.
- If you cannot find a phone, skip and keep moving. Volume of **reachable** accounts matters.

**Exercise:** 50 names on day 1, 50 on day 2, 50 on day 3, 50 on day 4. Day 5: clean duplicates and missing phones.

---

## Lesson 3 — First touches are learning + permission

Week 3 is not “close 10k.” Week 3 is: they hear a human, you hear a human.

Use Vault `cold-call-pl.md` and `cold-dm-pl.md`. Goal of first touch: **conversation or a booked callback**, not a pitch dump.

Daily target: **50 touches** (call, voicemail, SMS/email follow-up counts as part of a sequence, not 50 random channels).

Log everything. Unlogged work did not happen.

---

## Lesson 4 — Sequences, not one-and-done

Minimum sequence per account:

1. Call
2. Voicemail (short)
3. SMS or email same day if you have it
4. Call again 2 days later
5. Breakup message day 7

Stop if they say no. “Not now” gets a date in the sheet, not a sulk.

---

## Weekly ship

200-row list (screenshot of counts is fine). 50+ touches. 3 sentences: what objection you heard most.
