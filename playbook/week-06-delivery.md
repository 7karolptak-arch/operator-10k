# Week 6 — Delivery

**Ship:** a written delivery SOP for your offer. If you have a client: first-week onboarding done. If not: run a paid or heavily scoped pilot using the SOP anyway (even a friend-of-a-friend company at a real price, not free “for the portfolio”).

**War Room:** chaos, over-promising, no updates.

---

## Lesson 1 — Delivery is how you get the second month

Sales without delivery is a hobby. Delivery without a fence is how you work 80 hours for 1,500 zł.

SOP minimum:

- Kickoff (30 min): access, goals, what done looks like
- Weekly update (written, same day each week): done / next / stuck
- End of 30 days: recap + invoice + continue/kill

Vault: `onboarding.md`, `weekly-client-update.md`.

---

## Lesson 2 — Communication beats extra tactics

Clients fire silence faster than imperfect ads.

Rule: if something slips, you write first. Same day.

Do not hide in “I’m waiting on Meta / the designer / inspiration.”

---

## Lesson 3 — Scope fence

When they add “and also TikTok”:

“To jest osobny job. Mogę wycenić od następnego cyklu. W tym 30-dniowym zostajemy przy [zakres].”

If you cannot say that sentence, you will hate this work.

---

## Lesson 4 — Proof without fake testimonials

Save: before/after metrics you actually moved, screenshots of process, a quote you have permission to use. No invented 10k months. No “my client made 10k” unless it is true and they agree.

Your Operator scorecard is separate from client vanity.

---

## Weekly ship

SOP posted. If client exists: one weekly update sample (anonymized). Hours spent vs. price — are you underwater? If yes, cut scope next cycle, do not “hustle harder” as the only lever.
