## What you bought

You bought a **sequence** and a **room**.

- **The Playbook** — this classroom. One week is not another week. Do them in order.
- **The War Room** — weekly live. You bring a bottleneck. You leave with a next action.
- **Operator Network** — the feed. Post work, not vibes.
- **The Vault** — scripts and templates so you are not inventing documents.

You did **not** buy guaranteed income, a done-for-you agency, or someone who will call companies for you.

**10,000 PLN/month is the target the system is built around.** Results depend on execution, market, skill, and consistency.

## The vehicle (non-negotiable)

You are building a **B2B service business**: you sell a useful outcome to companies that already spend money. Typical first version: a marketing or operations service (leads, content system, ads ops, local SEO, appointment setting — one of these, not five).

You are **not** building: dropshipping, a personal brand as the product, a SaaS, a crypto play, or “whatever is hot this week.”

Why this vehicle: no inventory, no audience required, low cash to start, real buyers, skills that transfer.

## Rules of the Founding Class

1. One market. One offer. 60 days.
2. You talk to businesses every week after Week 2. Volume is part of the job.
3. Rejection is in the job description. It is data, not a verdict.
4. Post your weekly numbers in the Network: touches, conversations, meetings, offers, cash in.
5. Bring **one** bottleneck to War Room. Not your life story.
6. Do not promise clients outcomes you cannot control. Same rule we use on you.
