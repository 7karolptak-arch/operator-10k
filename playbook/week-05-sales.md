# Week 5 — Sales

**Ship:** run at least 3 discovery conversations with a structure. Make at least 2 explicit offers (price said out loud).

**War Room:** “They liked me but nothing happened” / “I freeze on price.”

---

## Lesson 1 — Sales is diagnosis, then a commercial sentence

You are not performing. You are checking fit and proposing a job.

Structure (Vault `discovery.md`):

1. Context — how they get customers now
2. Problem — where it breaks
3. Impact — time / money / stress (their numbers, even rough)
4. Attempts — what they already burned
5. Decision — who says yes, timing, cash
6. Fit — you say yes/no
7. Offer — one-pager out loud + price + deposit + start date
8. Next step — signed / paid / or a dated no

If you skip 7 and 8, it was a chat.

---

## Lesson 2 — Saying the number

Silence after the price is allowed. Do not decorate.

“Projekt na 30 dni: 2 500 zł netto / brutto [jak pracujesz]. 50% na start, reszta po odbiorze. Mogę zacząć w [data].”

If they flinch: “Na co patrzysz — zakres, timing, czy kwota?” Then shut up.

Discounting to “get the logo” trains them and you. If you discount, remove scope, do not smile it away.

---

## Lesson 3 — Objections (patterns)

- **“Send an email.”** → “Wyślę 8 zdań. Zostajemy przy [dzień/godzina] na 10 minut po przeczytaniu?”
- **“I have to think.”** → “O czym konkretnie? Żebym nie zgadywał.”
- **“Too expensive.”** → “Wobec czego? Ostatnia agencja / etat / nicnierobienie.”
- **“We already have someone.”** → “Super. Gdzie to nadal pęka? Jeśli nigdzie — odpuszczam.”
- **“Guaranteed leads/revenue?”** → “Nie gwarantuję Wam przychodu. Gwarantuję robotę i output z one-pagera. Jak ktoś obiecuje Wam 10k z reklam, nie podpisujcie.”

You will use the same honesty Operator uses with you.

---

## Lesson 4 — Paper

Before money moves: one-page agreement (scope, price, deposit, what “done” means, communication channel). Vault `simple-agreement.md`. Not a 20-page law firm novel. Not a DM handshake.
