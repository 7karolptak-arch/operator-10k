# Discovery call

Timebox: 20–30 minutes.

1. Context — how customers arrive
2. Break — where leads/jobs die
3. Impact — time / money / stress (their rough numbers)
4. Attempts — what they already paid for
5. Decision — who signs, timing, budget band
6. Fit — you accept or decline
7. Offer — one-pager + price + deposit + start
8. Next step — paid / dated no / specific homework

Price sentence: say the number, then silence.

Not a fit: “I would waste your money. Here is who to hire instead.” (one sentence, then leave)
