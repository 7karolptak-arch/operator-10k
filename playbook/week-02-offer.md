# Week 2 — Offer

**Ship:** one-pager: who, problem, outcome, price, what is included, what is not. Posted.

**War Room:** “Nobody would buy this” / “I don’t know what to charge.”

---

## Lesson 1 — Offers are products, not vibes

An offer is a **commercial object**:

- Who it is for (ICP from Week 1)
- The problem in their words
- The outcome in 30 days (specific, observable)
- Mechanism (what you actually do)
- Price and payment
- Scope fence (what you will not do)

If any line is missing, you do not have an offer. You have a bio.

---

## Lesson 2 — Build a 30-day paid job, not a retainer fantasy

First operators die on “monthly social media management” because scope is infinite and proof is mushy.

Better first offers (pick one shape, customize to your market):

- **Rescue:** “Inbox + follow-up for ad leads, 30 days, response under X hours.”
- **Install:** “Google Business + offer page + 20-touch local list, done in 14 days.”
- **Appointments:** “10 booked consults from outbound in 30 days” (only if you can control volume; never guarantee revenue).
- **Ops:** “Weekly numbers + one bottleneck process documented and run.”

Promise **work and a defined output**, not their revenue. You do not control their close rate.

---

## Lesson 3 — Pricing for a first operator

You are not 997/hour. You are also not 200 zł “because I’m new.” Cheap signals amateur and attracts babysitting.

**Starting band (Poland, first B2B service, 2026):** 1,500–4,000 zł for a 30-day defined project, or 1,500–3,000 zł/month for a narrow retainer with a kill-switch after 30 days.

Rules:

- Price the **job**, not your age.
- Collect a deposit before work (30–50%).
- Write what happens if they ghost (pause, not free extras).
- If they ask for “brand strategy + ads + TikTok + website,” that is three offers. Sell one.

**Exercise:** Write three prices: too cheap (you resent it), too scary (you won’t say it), the one you will actually say this week. Use the middle-high one.

---

## Lesson 4 — One-pager structure (Vault)

Copy `offer-one-pager.md`. Fill every heading. Read it out loud. If you need a second breath to explain it, cut.

Then write the spoken version (20 seconds): “I work with [ICP] who [problem]. In 30 days I [outcome] by [mechanism]. It’s [price]. If that’s relevant, I’ll ask you 6 questions and tell you if I can help.”
