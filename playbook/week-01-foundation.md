# Week 1 — Foundation

**Ship by end of week:** one market, one ICP, one problem in their words. Posted in the Network.

**War Room this week:** “My market is too wide” or “I picked something cute instead of something that pays.”

---

## Lesson 1 — Reduce the menu

The internet sold you optionality. Optionality is why you have zero clients.

You pick **one** industry you can reach without a following: businesses you can list from Google, maps, LinkedIn, or local knowledge. Examples that work for a first operator: dental / med, local services, e-commerce shops already selling, B2B trades, agencies that need overflow.

**Filter (all must be true):**

- They already spend money to get customers or save time.
- You can find 200+ of them in a week of list-building.
- You can explain their problem in one sentence without jargon.
- You are willing to call them.

**Not a market:** “anyone who wants to grow,” “coaches,” “everyone with Instagram.”

**Exercise:** Write 5 candidate markets. Kill 4 with the filter. Keep 1. If you cannot kill 4, your taste is the problem — pick the boring one with the most Google Maps pins.

## Lesson 3 — The problem in their language

You do not sell “marketing.” You sell a problem they already feel.

Bad: “I help brands scale with AI-driven funnels.”
Good: “You pay for ads and the inbox is a mess, so leads die after hours.”

Call 5 businesses this week **only to learn** (not to sell). Script is in the Vault (`research-call-pl.md`). Ask:

1. How do new customers find you?
2. Where do leads die?
3. What did you try that wasted money?
4. If a junior did one job well for 30 days, what job?

Write 10 phrases they actually said. Your Week 2 offer will steal this language.

---

## Lesson 4 — Vehicle honesty

A B2B service is: diagnose → propose a paid outcome → deliver → invoice.

You will prospect. You will be ignored. You will be bad at the start. That is the training.

If you refuse to speak to strangers, stop here and request a refund if your checkout allows it. This program will not replace that skill with content.
