## Lesson 1 — 10K is arithmetic, not a vision board

10,000 PLN/month **target** (not a promise):

Examples (illustrative):

- 4 clients × 2,500 zł
- 2 clients × 3,000 zł + 4,000 zł project
- 10,000 zł one project (harder to repeat)

Write **your** mix from the offer you actually sell, at the close rate you actually have (be ugly-honest). If you close 1 in 20 conversations, you can backsolve required conversations.

If the math needs 400 dials/week and you have 6 hours, the offer, ICP, or hours must change. Magic will not.

---

## Lesson 2 — Repeatable > impressive

Keep: the market, the one-pager, the sequence, the SOP, the weekly rhythm.

Kill: extra niches, extra services, content for its own sake, “personal brand as prerequisite.”

You may add a second offer **after** one is repeating. Not this week.

---

## Lesson 3 — After Founding Class

The identity: someone who can create an offer, find demand, sell, deliver, repeat.

Options:

- Stay in War Room / Network on the monthly (997 list) if that is how OPERATOR sells continuation.
- Run the cadence alone with the Vault.
- Raise price when you are full, by cutting scope for new clients or adding a waitlist — not by becoming a guru.

Do not teach this program to others as a knockoff while you still cannot fill a week of outreach.
