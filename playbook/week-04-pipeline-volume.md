# Week 4 — Pipeline (volume)

**Ship:** 100+ additional touches. At least 8 live conversations. At least 2 booked next steps (callback or meeting).

**War Room:** avoidance, thin lists, scripts that sound like a podcast.

---

## Lesson 1 — Volume is a skill, not a personality

If you did 50 touches once and then “worked on the brand,” you left the program.

This week you protect a **daily outreach block** (60–90 minutes). Phone in hand. Sheet open. No YouTube.

Track: dials, conversations, next steps. Conversations / dials will be ugly. Improve the list and the first 10 seconds, not your self-esteem.

---

## Lesson 2 — First 10 seconds

They decide if you are a vendor-bot or a person.

Bad: “Hi I’m X from Y and we help companies scale using cutting-edge…”
Good: “Cześć, [Name], Karol — krótko: dzwonię do [typ firm] w [miasto], bo [problem w ich języku]. Czy mam 20 sekund, czy lepiej oddzwonić po 16?”

If they grant 20 seconds, say the offer sentence from Week 2 and ask **one** diagnostic question.

---

## Lesson 3 — Gatekeepers

Reception is a role, not an enemy. Honesty + brevity.

“Nie sprzedaję abonamentu na cold call. Chcę 20 sekund z osobą, która ogarnia [leady / wizyty / reklamy]. Kto to u Was?”

If they want email: send the one-pager paragraph, then call again. Email-only is a stall until you treat it as a step, not a win.

---

## Lesson 4 — Repair the machine

Mid-week, look at the sheet:

- No conversations → your list is wrong or you are not actually calling.
- Conversations, zero next steps → you are talking, not booking. Ask for a time.
- Next steps that ghost → same-day SMS confirm + calendar invite if they have email.

Do not rebuild the offer this week unless War Room says the offer is unbuyable. Most pain is volume and booking.

---

## Weekly ship

Numbers: dials, conversations, next steps. One call recap (what you said, what they said, what you will change). No need to post audio.
