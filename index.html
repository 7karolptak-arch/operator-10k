12 — FAQ

Straight answers.

What exactly is Operator 10K?

An 8-week execution program that takes you from no offer to a working B2B service business: market, offer, pipeline, sales, delivery, and a weekly operating system — aimed at a 10,000 PLN/month target.

Do I need any experience?

No. You need the willingness to talk to businesses and follow the sequence. Experience is what the eight weeks are for.

Do I need money to start?

The vehicle is chosen because startup cost is low. You don’t need inventory or ads to begin prospecting. You will need time and a way to communicate with people.

Can I do this while I’m still in school?

Yes. The model is built to run alongside school or university. It still requires consistent weekly execution, not leftover minutes.

What business will I build?

A B2B service business — typically a marketing or operations service sold to companies. One vehicle. The Playbook helps you construct the specific offer.

What if I don’t know sales?

That’s expected. Sales is taught as a skill: conversations, diagnosis, structure, scripts, and live correction in The War Room.

What happens inside The War Room?

Weekly live coaching. You bring bottlenecks — offer, outreach, closing, delivery — and leave with a fix and a next action, instead of guessing for three weeks.

What is The Vault?

Implementation assets: scripts, templates, checklists, and systems so you can execute without inventing every document from scratch.

How much time should I expect to put in?

Treat it like building a real business, not watching a course. Plan for serious weekly work: lessons plus outreach, calls, and delivery. Exact hours depend on you — results follow volume of quality execution.

Is 10,000 PLN/month guaranteed?

No. 10K/month is the program target, not guaranteed income. We provide the system, feedback, and environment. Execution, market, and consistency are yours.

What happens after the 60 days?

You should have a defined offer, a prospecting motion, a sales process, and a delivery rhythm. The identity of an Operator is meant to continue after the cohort calendar ends.

What does Founding Access include?

Entry into the first 25-person Founding Class: Operator 10K, The Playbook, The War Room, Operator Network, The Vault, and 50% off the first month. Exact ongoing price will be stated at checkout when it is set.
